# Google Flow prompt pack — Vivaah

Rules of use:

1. **The style preamble below is pasted verbatim at the top of every prompt.**
   Never paraphrase it. It is the only reason six separately generated clips
   will look like one shoot.
2. Generate at the **highest resolution your plan allows**, 16:9.
3. **Audio is discarded**, so ignore it. Do not prompt for sound.
4. Return the raw downloads. Do not trim, crop or colour them; that happens here
   with ffmpeg, because scrub video needs a dense keyframe interval and a normal
   web encode scrubs like mud.
5. **Women only.** No male models, no groom, no couples. Project rule.
6. If a clip contains a visible watermark, say so and keep it anyway. It gets
   cropped or overlaid, exactly as the existing KlingAI hero frames were.
7. Reroll freely. Generation is the cheap part; a bad frame shipped is not.

---

## THE STYLE PREAMBLE (verbatim, every prompt)

> Shot on a full-frame cinema camera, 50mm prime, T2.0, shallow depth of field.
> A quiet Indian bridal atelier: soft cool north light from a large window
> camera-left, deep gentle falloff, no hard speculars, no coloured gels. The
> ground is warm porcelain white and pale grey plaster. Everything in frame is
> desaturated and neutral except the garment or metal, which is the only
> saturated object in the shot. Deep violet and antique gold appear only as
> small accents, never as the dominant colour of the frame. Immaculate,
> uncluttered, museum-like. No props beyond what is named. No text, no
> lettering, no signage, no logos, no watermarks. No people other than the women
> described. Unhurried, still, reverent. Photorealistic, not illustrated, not
> 3D-rendered, not stylised.

## CLIP 01 — HERO. "the atelier holds its breath"

Purpose: the opening. Must survive any grammar we choose, so generate this first.

> [STYLE PREAMBLE]
>
> A single bridal lehenga on a tailor's dress form, centred in a tall empty
> studio. Deep violet silk, dense antique-gold zari embroidery across the hem,
> the skirt falling in heavy folds to a pale stone floor. Fine dust drifting
> slowly through the window light. The room is otherwise completely empty.
>
> Camera: a very slow, steady dolly push straight in toward the garment, moving
> perhaps one metre across the whole shot. Locked horizon, no handheld shake, no
> pan, no tilt, no cut, no speed change. The move is continuous from the first
> frame to the last and never arrives.

Generate **three variations.** This one carries the page and is worth the rerolls.

## CLIP 02 — CRAFT MACRO. "the hand that made it"

Purpose: the Substance beat. Texture at a scale the eye cannot otherwise get.

> [STYLE PREAMBLE]
>
> Extreme macro on a woman's hands working gold zari thread into deep violet
> silk stretched on a wooden embroidery frame. The needle enters and draws
> through. Individual metallic filaments catch the light and separate. The
> weave of the silk is visible at thread level. Only the hands and the frame are
> in shot, cropped at the wrist, no face, no body.
>
> Camera: locked off, almost static, with only the faintest slow drift closer.
> The movement in frame comes from the hands, not the camera. No rack focus, no
> cut.

---

## CLIP 03 — JEWELLERY. "the vault opens"

Purpose: the jewellery room. Metal, not fabric, so the page changes material.

> [STYLE PREAMBLE]
>
> A shallow velvet-lined tray of antique gold Indian bridal jewellery laid out
> in rows: a heavy necklace, jhumka earrings, a maang tikka, bangles. Deep
> violet velvet beneath. The gold is warm and slightly worn, not mirror-bright,
> not new. Nothing is being held or worn.
>
> Camera: a slow lateral tracking move from left to right across the tray, the
> pieces passing through the light one at a time. Perfectly level, constant
> speed, no acceleration, no cut, no rotation.
